Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026  ·  Operator: Griffin Spence

Overview

Run the Ball is a sports trivia app. This policy describes what data the app collects, why, and how it is handled. We collect only what is necessary to run the game.

Data We Collect

  • Anonymous session identifier. When you open the app for the first time, an anonymous account is automatically created. No email address or password is required. This identifier is stored on your device and on our servers.
  • Username (optional). You may choose a display name. Usernames are visible to other players on the leaderboard. You are not required to set one; the app assigns a temporary placeholder until you choose.
  • Gameplay data. Your question answers, scores, correct/incorrect streaks, and XP earned are stored on our servers so your progress persists between sessions and contributes to leaderboard rankings.
  • Notification reminders (optional). If you allow notifications, the app schedules daily challenge reminders locally on your device. No push token or notification data is sent to our servers. You can turn notifications off in iOS Settings at any time.
  • Advertising data (via Google AdMob). The app displays ads served by Google AdMob. Before any tracking, the app asks for your permission through the iOS App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you allow it, Google may use your device advertising identifier (IDFA) to show more relevant ads. If you decline, ads are not personalized based on tracking. In either case, Google may collect your IP address and device information to serve and measure ads. See the Google Privacy Policy linked below for details. You can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your real name, email address (unless you voluntarily provide one to link your account), phone number, precise location, contacts, or camera/microphone access.

How We Use Your Data

  • To run the game: persist scores, streaks, and daily challenges across sessions.
  • To power leaderboards: display your username and score rank to other players.
  • To show ads: support free access to the game through ad revenue (Google AdMob).
  • To send notifications: deliver daily challenge reminders if you have opted in.

Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — database and authentication provider. Your game data and account are stored on Supabase infrastructure. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob — advertising network. Subject to Google's data collection practices. Google Privacy Policy

Data Retention

Your account data is retained as long as your account exists. You can request deletion by contacting us at the address below. Advertising data retention is governed by Google's policies.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
